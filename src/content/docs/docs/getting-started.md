This page helps you understand Sportykore's main roles before you create, manage, or join a competition.

## Before you start

- You can browse public competitions without signing in.
- You need to sign in before you create, manage, favourite, join, or edit anything.

## Steps

1. Open Sportykore and complete onboarding.
2. Browse the home feed, search, or country pages to see public competitions.
3. Sign in with an email code when the app asks you to.
4. If you are an organizer, open **Create** or **Manage**.
5. If you are a player, open an invite link or enter an invite code from your organizer.

## Roles

### Public viewer

Public viewers can open public competition, match, team, player, country, standings, stats, bracket, venue, and lineup pages without signing in.

### Competition organizer

The organizer owns a competition. Organizers can create and edit competitions, seasons, teams, venues, fixtures, stages, scores, stats, invites, and roster rows.

### Team admin

A team admin is assigned by the organizer. Team admins can manage lineups for their own assigned team. They do not get full competition management access.

### Player

Players join through invite links or codes that expire after 7 days. When a player accepts an invite, Sportykore creates or connects their player profile so their team, season, match, career stats, highlights, and awards can appear publicly.

## Rules & good to know

- Public read routes do not require sign-in.
- Favouriting, creating, managing, joining, and lineup editing require sign-in.
- Organizer-only actions are checked against competition ownership.
- Team admins are scoped to their assigned team.
- Player profile creation can happen through invite acceptance or the account profile area.
